Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

English·עברית

The short version

PakaText collects nothing. There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK in the app.

The developer runs no server. There is nowhere for your reading to be sent to, because there is no "there".

PakaText turns books and documents you own into audio. It does that work on services you hold accounts with, using API keys you supply. Your material goes from your device to those services and back. It never passes through anything belonging to the developer.

Where your library lives

Everything PakaText makes — the recognised text, the audio, the covers, your marks and notes, and the original photographs or files if you choose to keep them — is stored on your device and in your own private iCloud container. That container belongs to your Apple Account. The developer cannot see it, list it or reach it.

Your API keys are kept in your device's Keychain. They are sent to the service they belong to and to nowhere else.

What leaves your device, and where it goes

Every outbound request below is made by your device, directly, under your own credentials where credentials are involved.

Each of those services handles what it receives under its own terms and privacy policy, and the usage is billed to your own account with them. Read them: Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Google.

Device permissions

  • Camera — only to photograph pages of a book or an article. The photographs are stored with the item, and are sent to Anthropic to be read.
  • Photo library — only to let you pick pictures of pages you already took.

PakaText does not ask for contacts, location, microphone, health data or anything else, because it has no use for them.

Payment

PakaText is free for a small library and is unlocked by a single one-off purchase. The purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The developer never sees your name, your payment method or any part of your Apple Account — only Apple's confirmation that this device is entitled to the unlocked app.

The purchase buys the app. It does not pay for the recognition or the narration; those remain billed to your own accounts with Anthropic and ElevenLabs.

Children

PakaText is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Deleting things

Deleting an item in the app removes its text, its audio and its marks from your device and from your iCloud container. Deleting the app and its iCloud data removes everything. There is no copy anywhere else to ask about, because none was ever made.

What the services above have already received is governed by their own policies, and you control it through your own account with each of them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

תשלום

PakaText חינמית לספרייה קטנה, ונפתחת בתשלום חד-פעמי אחד. הרכישה מתבצעת כולה אצל Apple. המפתח לא רואה את שמך, לא את אמצעי התשלום שלך ולא שום חלק מחשבון ה-Apple שלך — רק את האישור של Apple שלמכשיר הזה יש זכאות.

הרכישה קונה את האפליקציה. היא לא משלמת על הזיהוי ועל ההקראה; אלה ממשיכים להיות מחויבים בחשבונות שלך ב-Anthropic וב-ElevenLabs.

מה שהשירותים שלמעלה כבר קיבלו כפוף למדיניות שלהם, ואתה שולט בו דרך החשבון שלך אצל כל אחד מהם.
